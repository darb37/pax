Read closely. Think critically. Use evidence. Your mission is to understand what Pax experiences—and what he cannot understand about the humans he loves.

Mission briefing

20–25 minutes

This activity is based on the excerpt from Pax by Sara Pennypacker. Complete the multiple-choice questions first, then respond to the open-ended prompts in your own words.

For written answers, explain your reasoning and refer to a specific moment, detail, or phrase from the excerpt. Your teacher will review these responses.
